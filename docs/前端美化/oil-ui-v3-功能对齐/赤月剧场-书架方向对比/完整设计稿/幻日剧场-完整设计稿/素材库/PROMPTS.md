## 人物 · 众相剧场

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Produce ONE new standalone background illustration for the CHARACTERS module of a novel-writing studio, titled conceptually "The theatre of identities". NO rendered text.
STYLE CONTRACT: exquisite handcrafted colored-paper bas-relief illustration, tangible thick matte paper fibers, razor-fine cut contours, folded and embossed surfaces, precise narrow antique-brass edge accents used sparingly, sophisticated engraved geometric micro-patterns, visibly separated foreground/midground/background planes with real occlusion and long soft cast shadows; dramatic soft raking warm light from upper left; refined literary editorial fantasy, not flat vector icons, not glossy plastic, not photoreal humans. Full-bleed 16:9 landscape art asset with clear focal hierarchy and rich harmonious color.
PRIMARY MOTIFS ARE EXCLUSIVELY HUMAN IDENTITY AND DRAMA: three individual theatrical face masks, paper-cut human profile shadows, fine vermilion relationship threads and overlapping stage wings.
Build a striking sculptural composition occupying the left and middle two thirds. The largest mask is in the lower-left foreground, three-quarter view, with a dignified thoughtful expression, ivory and deep ultramarine paper planes making its facial structure. Behind and above it a smaller terracotta-and-ochre mask turns in profile toward a third pale-jade mask facing the opposite way in a distant plane. These must have markedly different expressions and silhouettes, suggesting different personalities; quiet humane expressions, not creepy or horror. Mask eyes are beautifully sculpted narrow eyelids, not empty black holes. No carnival sequins or feathers. Thin vermilion threads pass between masks and the layered human silhouette shadows behind them, as an understated metaphor for relationships and intertwined character arcs. At the upper left a small papercraft puppeteer's wooden cross partially enters the composition to hold a few threads; integrated and elegant, not torture imagery.
The stage is formed by dramatically sweeping folded-paper wings in cobalt, muted jade, warm rust and cream; use irregular theatrical contours, depth and diagonals, no squared panels. A close foreground wing overlaps one mask, the intermediate masks cast clearly separated shadows on the more distant silhouettes, then larger backdrop folds recede farther. At least seven depth layers. The RIGHTMOST quarter opens into calm desaturated blue-gray paper with sparse overlapping shadow edges, naturally usable as UI breathing room, not a framed rectangle or a blank beige blob. It is still part of the dimensional stage.
Color: ultramarine/cobalt, terracotta, ochre, muted jade, warm ivory, vermilion thread; small dark-bronze edge accents only. Detailed, tactile and elegant; visually rich through layered form and contrasting colors rather than gold filigree.
STRICT EXCLUSIONS: absolutely no flowers, vines, leaves, ginkgo, branches, botanical borders, butterflies, birds, moons, mountains, pagodas, palaces, floating islands, books, scrolls, pens, traditional costume figures, jewelry frames, arch windows, optical prisms or lenses. No text, letters, numbers, logos, UI cards, contact sheets, grids or watermarks. New visual vocabulary, not a floral landscape with subjects swapped.
```
