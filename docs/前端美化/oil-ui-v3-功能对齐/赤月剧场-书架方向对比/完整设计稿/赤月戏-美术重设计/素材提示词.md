# 布景素材与提示词

三张素材均使用内置 imagegen 生成，未使用 CLI 或其他图像服务。图像不含 UI 文字与控件；最终保存为本目录 `assets/secession.webp`、`assets/mucha.webp`、`assets/rinpa.webp`。三个独立 HTML 使用真实中文字体与文本。

## ① secession.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. A masterfully crafted vertical decorative theatrical mural for the left 28 percent of a luxury Chinese novelist writing workspace, no interface and absolutely no lettering. Viennese Secession inspired, Klimt gold leaf and geometric mosaic artistry, deeply sophisticated antique gold, ivory and near-black plum. Tall monumental composition 2:3 portrait. A large eclipsed vermilion moon near upper third, sinuous luminous golden branches and tiny enamel squares, two elegant pale stylized swans below, a dark folded velvet stage at base. Surfaces show physical gold leaf seams, irregular hand-laid mosaic, hairline engraved contours, subtle warm stage lighting. Ornament density concentrated to left edge and lower third; upper right is calm near-black charcoal for real HTML small title to overlay. Premium museum exhibition scenic panel, exquisite artistry and precise hand-finished materials, serious and rich rather than cute or game UI. Flat frontal mural with tangible depth at edges. No human figures, no product mockup, no words, no logos, no watermark. Opaque image, no transparency.

## ② mucha.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Elaborate bespoke Art Nouveau jewellery-theatre scenic panel, tall 2:3 portrait, suitable as right-side artwork in a luxury literary writing workspace. Alphonse Mucha inspired decorative framing and stained glass craftsmanship, but original composition with a burgundy red moon behind a stylized ivory moth and cascading abstract orchid petals. Dark midnight sapphire navy background, wine-red translucent enamel, smoky pearl, restrained polished rose gold. Elegant large horseshoe arch, sinuous bronze lines and precisely inset jewel glass with bevels, flowing petal silhouettes. Top half has a striking red lunar glass medallion and pale moth; lower quarter fades to clear deep blue-black for real UI overlay. Exquisitely detailed museum quality lithograph meets handcrafted stained glass, restrained metallic glints, dramatic sidelight, no neon. Ornament follows structural curves, refined varying line widths. No words, no lettering, no humans, no interface, no buttons, no logos, no watermark. Opaque image.

## ③ rinpa.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. A sophisticated panoramic Japanese Rinpa theatre backdrop for a literary writing studio. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. Layered ancient gold-leaf folding-screen panels, charcoal lacquer lower edges, a large muted blood-red lunar disc at upper left, dramatic sweeping white plum blossom branches from upper right, sparse ivory blossoms, tiny specks of gold dust and beautiful visible hand-laid gold-leaf seams. Deep ink-black near the far left and right edges, warm pale gold centre with deliberately large clean negative space. Expressive asymmetrical screen composition with subtle theatrical stage shadows, flat decorative painting and highly tactile gilded surface. No neon, no lens flares, no busy continuous flower wallpaper. Museum masterwork quality, atmospheric and luxurious. Do not depict a room, desk or product. No text, no letters, no symbols, no UI, no logo, no watermark. Opaque image.
