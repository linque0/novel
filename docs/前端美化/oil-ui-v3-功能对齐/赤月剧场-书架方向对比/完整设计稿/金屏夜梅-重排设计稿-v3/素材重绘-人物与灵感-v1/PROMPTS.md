# 生成记录

模式：内置 `image_gen`。两张均为新图生成，参考图仅用于风格与工艺，不是修改目标。未使用 CLI 或 API 回退。

## 人物：众生织幕

参考：`../assets/nouveau.webp`（v3 书架素材）。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: high-quality landscape illustrated background artwork for the CHARACTERS module of a Chinese novel-writing studio. This is an ART ASSET, no interface, no text.
Reference image 1 is STYLE AND CRAFT REFERENCE ONLY: match its exquisitely crafted multilayer cut-paper diorama, tactile matte colored paper, thin warm gold-foil edges and fine engraving, Eastern literary + Art Nouveau botanical linework, rich controlled colors, physical occlusion and cast shadows. Do not copy its landscape, red moon, arch border, or exact palette.
Create a completely new theme: "an ensemble of lives, intertwined destinies". A sculptural literary ensemble of three elegantly stylized human figures with clearly different profiles, ages and clothes, composed as an asymmetrical, deep paper theatre. An ink-blue cloaked young traveler in the nearer left-middle plane, an ochre/rust older storyteller in a separate middle plane, a sage and dusty-rose female figure farther right behind an irregular paper opening. Faces and garments are exquisitely simplified cut-paper illustration with engraved detail, not photos, not realistic plastic skin. Each figure must read as an individual, not duplicated silhouettes. A few thin copper-gold strands weave naturally between personal objects and clothing, evoking relationships; not a diagram, no node circles. Layered overlapping fabric-like paper folds and small literary keepsakes imply backstories. Curved art nouveau branch rhythms integrated into the scene, not a stock floral frame.
Composition: wide 16:9 full-bleed artwork, main ensemble across the center-left to center; keep a naturally quiet light parchment region occupying about the rightmost quarter for future UI content. Do not split into boxed zones. Irregular foreground paper folds overlap the midground figures, with a deep burgundy and midnight-blue recess behind them and softly receding peach/celadon layers. At least six visibly distinct depth planes, crisp cut edges, strongly legible cast shadows, foreground larger and detailed, background smaller and softened. Architectural-screen and ribbon shapes should recede in depth. No large moon or landscape valley.
Palette: rich ink blue, warm ochre, faded coral, sage green, mulberry, parchment ivory and restrained antique gold. Colorful like the reference, not monochrome gold on red, not teal-only. Quiet refined luxury, imaginative storytelling.
Lighting: warm soft raking light from upper left emphasizing raised paper edges and spatial layering. Consistent handmade illustration style throughout. Output one polished standalone landscape image. No words, letters, calligraphy, logos, watermarks, UI panels, buttons, portrait cards, grids, contact sheets, photorealism, game character imitation, glossy 3D render, cluttered jeweled ornament.
```

## 灵感：纸上生境

参考 1：`../assets/nouveau.webp`；参考 2：本轮 `characters-ensemble-v1.png`，以保持伴随素材的纸雕、金箔、线条和光照语言一致。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: landscape illustrated background artwork for the INSPIRATION CAPTURE module of a Chinese novel-writing studio. A standalone ART ASSET, absolutely no UI and no text.
Input image 1 is the existing bookshelf artwork, STYLE/CRAFT REFERENCE ONLY. Input image 2 is the newly designed characters-module artwork, a COMPANION STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. The new artwork must unmistakably belong to the SAME illustrated family: exceptionally intricate handcrafted multilayer cut-paper diorama, tangible matte paper fibers, thin warm gold-foil edges, finely engraved botanical and architectural detail, Eastern literary imagination + flowing Art Nouveau rhythms, rich intentional color, strong physical cast shadows and elegant spatial depth. Match craftsmanship, contour language, detail density and lighting; do not copy subjects or composition from either reference.
New theme: "a fleeting thought unfolds into a world". A beautifully sculpted open manuscript in the lower central-right foreground, its blank cut-paper pages curling upward into a luminous diagonal current of ribbons. The current opens into three small, unfinished story-world fragments suspended at DIFFERENT depths: a lavender and indigo miniature dream-city, a celadon and jade grove, an apricot and saffron cloud island. These are delicate paper sculptures coming alive out of the pages, not vignettes in boxes. Several paper butterflies and a few glowing golden seed-like flecks lift from the pages, representing captured sparks of ideas. A small elegant brass pen rests diagonally near the open book, integrated into the paper composition. All book surfaces blank, no marks that resemble text.
Composition MUST DIFFER from the companion characters scene: airy diagonal ascending / gently spiraling movement from lower-right manuscript toward upper-middle dream worlds, no row of people, no portrait arch, no central moon. Wide 16:9, full bleed. An irregular, calm warm-ivory paper area in the UPPER LEFT quarter leaves room for future interface content, with a few fine filigree tendrils at its organic edge. Not a rigid rectangular blank panel. Oversized near-field curled blue-violet paper leaves at bottom right partially occlude the book, layered pages in the near middle, worlds and butterflies suspended farther behind, and soft pale apricot/mauve paper cloud layers in the distance. At least six CLEARLY SEPARATE depth planes with visible thickness and cast shadows. Do not cover the artwork in an ornamental frame. Generous pockets of air between evocative objects.
Palette distinct from both references but harmonious: periwinkle and ultramarine, jade/celadon, warm saffron, peach coral, soft mauve, parchment ivory and restrained antique gold. More chromatic variety than a monochrome gold-on-dark design. Warm raking light from upper left should create the same matte paper and gold edge quality as the companion image.
Polished sophisticated editorial illustration for a literary creative studio. Imaginative, contemplative, sophisticated. No text, calligraphy, logos, watermarks, labels, interface, buttons, cards, grids, contact sheet, people, photographic skin, glossy plastic 3D render, giant red moon, symmetrical floral wreath, repeated characters composition. Output ONE complete landscape image.
```
